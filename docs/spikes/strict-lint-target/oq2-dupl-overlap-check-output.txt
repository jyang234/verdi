## Conclusion

The line-range-overlap check confirms, discriminately this time, exactly
what the withdrawn grep-based witness claimed by accident: dupl never
reports the hasDotDotElement pair at 1be75d01, at any threshold from the
default 150 down to 10. Combined with R-1's package-scope finding
(oq2-dupl-package-scope-output.txt: 0/37 of this same lane's own full-tree
dupl findings are cross-package, and the hasDotDotElement pair is itself
cross-package — cmd/verdi's `package main` vs internal/readinessload's
`package readinessload`), the mechanism is now the correct one: dupl runs
per package and structurally cannot see this pair, at any threshold,
regardless of the function's length.
